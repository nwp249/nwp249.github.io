Senior Design: Open-Source Car Head Unit

Description
An open-source double-DIN car head unit built on an embedded Linux single-board computer with a touchscreen interface. It supports multiple audio inputs (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, USB, analog) and channeled audio output with digital signal processing.
My role
I am in charge of designing the hardware stack, including the single-board computer choice, physical interface, and integration with the carrier board designed by my team members.
Skills and knowledge gained
Rust/C++, Slint, embedded Linux, system architecture, plugin-based software design, and infrastructure-as-code so outside contributors can reproduce the environment.

Big-picture contribution
The hobbyist car community has a need for an audio system that enthusiasts can adapt to their liking, which is where our product fits. It also contributes to the FOSH (free open-source hardware) community, a net positive.
