Career Objective

I am currently an undergraduate Computer Engineering student at Iowa State University, with interests in embedded systems and hardware design. After completing my undergraduate degree in May 2027, I plan to pursue a master's degree in Computer Engineering here at Iowa State. My primary goal is to contribute to a field that I hold close to my heart, such as agriculture technology. I spent a few summers as a teenager weeding corn and soybean test plots with my closest friends, and seeing all the huge machinery and complex applications of technology inspired me to pursue my education and focus on embedded systems design.
